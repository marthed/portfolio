About

I'm currently doing a Phd in HCI (Human-Computer Interaction) at the Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) in Stockholm. My main research topic is on physical locomotion techniques in mixed reality applications. Besides my main focus I have also developed side-projects within the Phd in the design of low-code platforms for web applications. Other interests include meta-science, philosophy, economics, neuroscience and animal (including human) behavior.

Teaching

At KTH I developed and hosted the course DH2643 Advanced Interaction Programming. The course is taught for computer science and media technology master students in their final year and teaches full-stack web application development, including front-end/back-end frameworks, deployment with docker and devops principles. I also served as the head teaching assistant in the preceding course DH2642 Interaction Programming, and have supervised 8 master thesis works.
